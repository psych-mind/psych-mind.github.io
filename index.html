Research

My PhD, Information Extraction from Large Knowledge Bases, focuses on temporal entity alignment: finding the nodes in different temporal knowledge graphs that refer to the same real-world entity. I am advised by Prof. Xue Li and Prof. Wen Hua, and supported by an Australian Government Research Training Program Scholarship.

  • Temporal and evolving knowledge graphs. Alignment across sources and time, incremental learning on dynamic graphs, and loss-grounded message weighting in graph neural networks.
  • Language models grounded in structured knowledge. Retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge-graph-grounded dialogue agents, and multi-hop reasoning.
  • Alignment and evaluation. When preference optimization beats supervised fine-tuning, and evaluation that stays calibrated and auditable without an LLM judge.

Publications

  1. EMNLP 2026

    ELITE: Evolving Localized and Informative Temporal Entity Alignment

    Jiayun Li, et al. Main Conference.

    The first framework for entity alignment on knowledge graphs that keep evolving. Informative seed selection and localized incremental inference give about 4× faster inference and 10× less memory than full retraining, at state-of-the-art accuracy.

    Code

  2. ACL 2026
    Findings

    RCTEA: Richness-guided Co-training for Temporal Entity Alignment

    Jiayun Li, et al. Findings of ACL 2026, pp. 39295–39310.

    Dual-view structural and temporal graph attention, weighted by how much evidence each neighbour carries, with neighbourhood-consensus denoising. About 6% over the strongest baselines on YAGO-WIKI180K and BETA.

    Code

  3. WSDM 2025

    HTEA: Heterogeneity-aware Embedding Learning for Temporal Entity Alignment

    Jiayun Li, et al. WSDM 2025, pp. 982–990.

    Shows that existing methods assume homogeneous structure and degrade on multi-source data, and proposes heterogeneity-aware embedding learning.

    Code

  4. Under review and in preparation
  5. IEEE TKDE

    Beyond Richness: Loss-Grounded Neighbourhood Utility for Temporal Entity Alignment

    Jiayun Li, et al. Under review.

    Rich neighbours are not always helpful ones: on BETA, the 2.34% highest-degree senders draw 48.49% of attention, yet removing their messages improves accuracy. RUNE-TEA learns each message's measured effect on the alignment loss and corrects richness-guided attention with it, improving H@1 over RCTEA by 2.35, 1.09, and 1.91 points under full-candidate evaluation.

  6. In prep.

    SPOT: Socratic Probing over Typed-graphs for Detecting Student Blind Spots

    Jiayun Li, et al. Targeting ACL 2027.

    A knowledge-graph-grounded Socratic agent that finds what a student doesn't know they don't know, with calibrated per-concept beliefs in place of a biased LLM judge.

    Code

SFT vs. DPO: when does preference optimization pay off?

A controlled LoRA study on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, measured only with judge-free metrics such as behavior rate, reference perplexity, and per-token KL.

off-policy pairs: ~4.2× KL cost · SFT works at N=50, DPO needs ~300

Oil & gas knowledge graph QA

Tiered retrieval-augmented question answering over real global energy asset data, with Neo4j and PostgreSQL backends. Each query is routed to templated retrieval, two-stage synthesis, or multi-hop retrieval to balance cost and latency.

14-table schema · 36-field curated dataset

Scaling alignment on H100 clusters
